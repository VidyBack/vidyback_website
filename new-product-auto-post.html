New Product Auto Post

Add a product to your store. VidyBack turns it into a video and posts it for you.

Every new product deserves a launch. But filming, editing and posting for each one takes hours you don't have. New Product Auto Post does it on its own: it spots new products in your store, makes a short video for each, and schedules it to your social channels.

How It Works

1

Connect your store and socials

Connect your store (Shopify, Jumpseller, Wix, Etsy or Ecwid) and the social accounts you want to post to. It takes a couple of minutes.

2

Turn on New Product Auto Post

Open Auto Post on your Home page and switch on New products. That's the only setup.

3

VidyBack watches for new products

Twice a day, VidyBack checks your store for products you've just added.

4

A video is made for each product

For every new product, VidyBack creates a short, on-brand video from your product photos, title and price. It also writes a caption with hashtags.

5

It's scheduled and posted

The videos are spread across the days ahead, up to 10 posts a day, so your feed stays active without flooding your followers.

FAQ

How often does VidyBack check for new products?

Twice a day, every day.

How many posts will go out?

Up to 10 posts per day. If you add more products than that, the rest are scheduled for the following days.

Can I stop a post before it goes live?

Yes. Use the Don't post button in the preview email.

Which platforms are supported?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest. Posts go only to the accounts you've connected.

What if a post fails on one platform?

The post is marked Partially posted and shows the reason. Fix the issue (for example, reconnect the account) and tap Repost.

Do I need design or video skills?

No. VidyBack builds the video and caption from your product details.

Can I turn it off?

Yes, any time, from Auto Post on your Home page.
