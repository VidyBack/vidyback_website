About VidyBack

AI Social Media Automation, Built for Ecommerce Stores

VidyBack exists to help Shopify, WooCommerce, Jumpseller and DTC brand owners turn their product catalog into scroll-stopping Reels, Stories, posts and portrait videos, without hiring a video editor or social media team.

Why we built VidyBack

Running an online store already means juggling inventory, orders, customer support and marketing. For most store owners, consistent social media content is the first thing that falls behind. Not because it doesn't matter, but because there's no time left in the day to write captions, edit videos and design posts for every new product.

VidyBack was built to close that gap. By connecting directly to your store catalog, VidyBack automatically generates branded Reels, Stories, posts and portrait or YouTube-size videos for your products, so your social presence stays active and consistent even when you can't get to it yourself.

Our focus stays narrow and specific: helping ecommerce and DTC brands turn products they already have into content they'd otherwise never get around to making.
