Cookies Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit our website. They help us recognize you when you return and improve your browsing experience. Cookies may be "persistent" (they remain on your device until you delete them) or "session" (they expire when you close your browser).

How We Use Cookies

VidyBack uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analyze website performance and user behavior
  • Improve our services based on user interactions
  • Provide personalized content recommendations
  • Track conversion events and campaign effectiveness

Purpose

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic functionality such as navigation and access to secure areas.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Analyzes website traffic and user behavior. Google Privacy Policy
  • PostHog Analytics: Tracks product usage and feature adoption. PostHog Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts: Delivers custom fonts for our website. No tracking cookies.

Your Cookie Choices

You have several options to control cookies:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. You can typically find cookie settings in your browser's "Options" or "Preferences" menu.
  • Disable Cookies: You can disable cookies entirely, but this may limit functionality on our website.
  • Delete Cookies: You can delete cookies that have already been set. Instructions vary by browser.
  • Opt-Out Tools: You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on

Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" feature. Currently, our website does not respond to DNT signals, but we respect your browser settings for cookie management.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review it regularly to stay informed about how we use cookies. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
